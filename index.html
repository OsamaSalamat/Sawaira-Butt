Hello, I'm Sawaira — a teacher, a mentor, and a lifelong learner.

I'm a Montessori and Computer Teacher based in Mandra, Pakistan, with over seven years of hands-on classroom experience. I've spent my career helping young children take their very first steps into education — teaching them not just letters and numbers, but confidence, discipline, and a genuine love for learning.
